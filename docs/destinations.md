# Destination Content and Sources

These are the 15 real CALABARZON destinations for the app (3 per province). Each entry is ready to copy into the app's **Add Destination** form. A source is listed for every fact.

**Before entering each one:**
- Read the **Check** notes. Those are details that sources disagree on or that change over time. Confirm them, or leave them out.
- **Photos:** use your own photos, or free-to-use photos from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org) (search the place name). Save the photo to your phone's gallery first, then pick it in the app.

## Cavite

### 1. Aguinaldo Shrine
- **Name:** Aguinaldo Shrine
- **Province:** Cavite
- **Category:** Historical Site
- **Address:** Tirona Highway, Brgy. Kaingen, Kawit, Cavite
- **Description:** The ancestral home of Gen. Emilio Aguinaldo, where Philippine independence was declared on June 12, 1898. It is now a museum managed by the National Historical Commission of the Philippines (NHCP).
- **Sources:** [Wikipedia: Aguinaldo Shrine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aguinaldo_Shrine), [Manila Bulletin](https://mb.com.ph/2019/06/12/memories-of-the-revolution-at-the-aguinaldo-shrine)

### 2. Corregidor Island
- **Name:** Corregidor Island
- **Province:** Cavite
- **Category:** Historical Site
- **Address:** Corregidor Island, Cavite City, Cavite
- **Description:** A small rocky island at the entrance of Manila Bay that was a key fortress in World War II. Its Malinta Tunnel, built by American engineers from 1922 to 1933, served as a wartime headquarters where President Manuel L. Quezon and Gen. Douglas MacArthur took shelter. Visitors usually go on an organized day tour.
- **Check:** Tour boat departure points have changed over the years (Manila or Mariveles, Bataan). Don't put a departure point in the description.
- **Sources:** [Showcaves: Malinta Tunnel](https://showcaves.com/english/ph/subterranea/Malinta.html), [Pacific Wrecks: Malinta Tunnel](https://img.pacificwrecks.com/location/philippines/cavite/corregidor/malinta/index.html), [Rappler](https://www.rappler.com/life-and-style/travel/48298-touring-corregidor-philippines-military-bastion/)

### 3. Museo De La Salle
- **Name:** Museo De La Salle
- **Province:** Cavite
- **Category:** Museum
- **Address:** De La Salle University-Dasmariñas, Dasmariñas City, Cavite
- **Description:** A lifestyle museum inside the DLSU-Dasmariñas campus that shows how wealthy, educated Filipinos (ilustrados) lived in the 19th century. Its bahay na bato-inspired building has rooms such as a prayer room, a master's bedroom, an office, and a dining hall, filled with period furniture and objects.
- **Check:** Visiting hours and entry rules on the museum's website.
- **Sources:** [Museo De La Salle (official)](https://www.dlsud.edu.ph/museodelasalle/collections/furniture.htm), [PTV News](https://ptvnews.ph/getting-know-ilustrado-lifestyle-museo-de-la-salle/)

## Laguna

### 4. Rizal Shrine
- **Name:** Rizal Shrine
- **Province:** Laguna
- **Category:** Historical Site
- **Address:** Corner F. Mercado St. and J.P. Rizal St., Brgy. 5 Poblacion, Calamba, Laguna
- **Description:** The birthplace of Dr. José Rizal, the national hero of the Philippines. The original house was destroyed in World War II; the present house was rebuilt on the same site in 1949 to 1950 with the help of National Artist Juan Nakpil. It is now a museum run by the NHCP, with Rizal family furniture, books, and memorabilia.
- **Sources:** [Wikipedia: Rizal Shrine (Calamba)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rizal_Shrine_(Calamba)), [Inquirer](https://business.inquirer.net/272607/retracing-rizals-steps)

### 5. Pagsanjan Falls
- **Name:** Pagsanjan Falls
- **Province:** Laguna
- **Category:** Nature & Park
- **Address:** Brgy. Anglas, Cavinti, Laguna
- **Description:** One of the most famous waterfalls in the Philippines, also called Magdapio Falls. Visitors ride small boats that boatmen paddle and push upstream, then shoot the rapids on the way back down. The falls are in Cavinti, but the classic boat ride starts in the town of Pagsanjan.
- **Check:** Boat ride fees change; don't include prices.
- **Sources:** [Lakwatsero](https://www.lakwatsero.com/spots/magdapio-falls-pagsanjan-falls-and-kipot-falls-on-a-budget/), [Wikipedia: Pagsanjan Falls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pagsanjan_Falls)

### 6. Nagcarlan Underground Cemetery
- **Name:** Nagcarlan Underground Cemetery
- **Province:** Laguna
- **Category:** Historical Site
- **Address:** Nagcarlan, Laguna
- **Description:** A Spanish-era Catholic cemetery built in 1845 under Franciscan priest Fr. Vicente Velloc. Beneath its chapel is an underground crypt that was used for Spanish friars and prominent town families. It is a National Historical Landmark, and no more burials are allowed there.
- **Check:** The street or barangay address. Sources also disagree on the year it became a landmark (1973 or 1981), so the year is left out.
- **Sources:** [Wikipedia: Nagcarlan Underground Cemetery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagcarlan_Underground_Cemetery), [Atlas Obscura](https://atlasobscura.com/places/nagcarlan-underground-cemetery)

## Batangas

### 7. Taal Heritage Town
- **Name:** Taal Heritage Town
- **Province:** Batangas
- **Category:** Historical Site
- **Address:** Calle Marcela Mariño Agoncillo, Taal, Batangas
- **Description:** A Spanish-era town with many well-preserved ancestral houses along its main street. Highlights include the Marcela Agoncillo house, home of the woman who sewed the first Philippine flag, and the blue-and-yellow Villavicencio "Wedding Gift" House.
- **Sources:** [Pinoy Travelogue: Taal Heritage Town guide](https://www.pinoytravelogue.com/2017/10/travel-guide-taal-heritage-town.html), [The Poor Traveler](https://www.thepoortraveler.net/2012/04/taal-heritage-town-ancestral-houses-batangas/), [Wikivoyage: Taal](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Taal)

### 8. Basilica of St. Martin de Tours (Taal Basilica)
- **Name:** Basilica of St. Martin de Tours
- **Province:** Batangas
- **Category:** Church / Religious Site
- **Address:** Taal, Batangas
- **Description:** The Minor Basilica of St. Martin of Tours, often called the largest Catholic church in Asia. It stands on a hill in the heritage town of Taal and is a National Historical Landmark.
- **Check:** The exact street address. Size figures differ between sources, so they are left out.
- **Sources:** [Wikipedia: Taal Basilica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taal_Basilica), [Benjie Layug](https://benjielayug.com/1999/10/basilica-of-st-martin-of-tours-taal-batangas.html)

### 9. Taal Volcano
- **Name:** Taal Volcano
- **Province:** Batangas
- **Category:** Nature & Park
- **Address:** Taal Lake, Batangas
- **Description:** One of the most active volcanoes in the Philippines: a volcano island in the middle of Taal Lake. PHIVOLCS does not allow anyone to enter Volcano Island, so visitors view it from a safe distance, such as the Tagaytay ridge.
- **Check:** PHIVOLCS kept Taal at Alert Level 1 through mid-2026 with minor eruptions. Check the current alert level at phivolcs.dost.gov.ph before presenting.
- **Sources:** [Manila Bulletin (June 30, 2026)](https://mb.com.ph/2026/06/30/taal-volcano-produces-minor-phreatomagmatic-eruptions-on-june-30), [Manila Bulletin (April 26, 2026)](https://mb.com.ph/2026/04/26/2-phreatic-eruptions-logged-at-taal-volcano-in-24-hours-phivolcs)

## Rizal

### 10. Hinulugang Taktak
- **Name:** Hinulugang Taktak
- **Province:** Rizal
- **Category:** Nature & Park
- **Address:** Taktak Road, Brgy. Dela Paz, Antipolo City, Rizal
- **Description:** A waterfall in Antipolo whose name comes from a legend about a church bell that fell there. It was declared a national park in 1990 and a protected landscape in 2000, and it reopened to visitors in 2020 after rehabilitation.
- **Sources:** [Rizal Province Tourism](https://rizalprovince.ph/pages/tourism-nature.html), [Wikipedia: Hinulugang Taktak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinulugang_Taktak), [GMA News](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/lifestyle/travel/441773/hinulugang-taktak-reopens-in-antipolo/story/)

### 11. Antipolo Cathedral
- **Name:** Antipolo Cathedral
- **Province:** Rizal
- **Category:** Church / Religious Site
- **Address:** Antipolo City, Rizal
- **Description:** The International Shrine of Our Lady of Peace and Good Voyage, home of the much-visited image of the Virgin Mary. The Vatican approved its international shrine status, and it was solemnly declared on January 26, 2024: the first international shrine in the Philippines.
- **Check:** The exact street address.
- **Sources:** [Inquirer](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/1616817/vatican-declares-antipolo-cathedral-international-shrine-first-in-ph/), [Philstar](https://www.philstar.com/headlines/2024/01/26/2328767/antipolo-cathedral-declared-international-shrine), [Wikipedia: Antipolo Cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antipolo_Cathedral)

### 12. Masungi Georeserve
- **Name:** Masungi Georeserve
- **Province:** Rizal
- **Category:** Nature & Park
- **Address:** Km. 47, Marcos Highway, Baras, Rizal
- **Description:** A conservation area of sharp limestone (karst) peaks and rainforest. Guided trails take visitors across the rocks on rope bridges and nets. Visits are by reservation only through the official website.
- **Check:** Fees change; don't include prices.
- **Sources:** [Wikipedia: Masungi Georeserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masungi_Georeserve), [Philippine News Agency](https://alpha.pna.gov.ph/articles/1122977), [Will Fly for Food](https://www.willflyforfood.net/the-masungi-georeserve-survival-guide/)

## Quezon

### 13. Kamay ni Hesus Shrine
- **Name:** Kamay ni Hesus Shrine
- **Province:** Quezon
- **Category:** Church / Religious Site
- **Address:** Brgy. Tinamnan, Lucban, Quezon
- **Description:** A pilgrimage site known for its 50-foot (15.2 m) statue of the Risen Christ on a hill, completed in 2002, which pilgrims reach by climbing a long stairway. Its church is known for healing Masses led by Fr. Joey Faller. It is busiest during Holy Week.
- **Sources:** [Wikipedia: List of tallest statues in the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_tallest_statues_in_the_Philippines), [Manila Bulletin](https://mb.com.ph/2023/4/7/thousands-visit-kamay-ni-jesus-healing-church-in-quezon), [GMA News](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/balitambayan/balita/866286/kamay-ni-hesus-shrine-sa-quezon-tampok-ang-50-ft-na-estatwa-ni-hesus-dinarayo-ngayong-semana-sant/story/)

### 14. Pahiyas Festival
- **Name:** Pahiyas Festival
- **Province:** Quezon
- **Category:** Festival & Cultural Event
- **Address:** Lucban, Quezon
- **Description:** Held every May 15 in honor of San Isidro Labrador, the patron saint of farmers. Houses along the procession route are decorated with kiping (colorful rice wafers), rice stalks, fruits, and vegetables as thanks for a good harvest.
- **Sources:** [Philippine News Agency](https://alpha.pna.gov.ph/articles/1174817), [Rappler](https://www.rappler.com/life-and-style/arts-culture/video-lucban-quezon-celebrates-pahiyas-2023-colorful-house-displays/), [UP Art Studies Journal (PDF)](https://artstudiesjournal.upd.edu.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/VOL1_ISS1_ARTICLE-5_PUNONGBAYAN-TAN-PEARL_THE-PAHIYAS-IN-LUCBAN.pdf)

### 15. Quezon Provincial Capitol
- **Name:** Quezon Provincial Capitol
- **Province:** Quezon
- **Category:** Landmark
- **Address:** Beside Perez Park, Lucena City, Quezon
- **Description:** An American-era capitol building from the early 1900s, designed by American architect William Parsons and later expanded by Filipino architect Juan Arellano. Its front still reads "Tayabas Provincial Capitol," the province's name before it was renamed after President Manuel L. Quezon.
- **Check:** The street address. Sources give 1906 or 1908 for its construction, so only "early 1900s" is used.
- **Sources:** [Inquirer: century-old Kapitolyo rehabilitation](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/?p=264930), [Lakwatserang Ligaw](https://www.lakwatserangligaw.com/2014/09/quezon-provincial-capitol.html)
